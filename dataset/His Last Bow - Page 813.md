Just listen with all your ears.” Then in an instant his sudden access of strength departed, and his masterful, purposeful talk droned away into the low, vague murmurings of a semi-delirious man. From the hiding-place into which I had been so swiftly hustled I heard the footfalls upon the stair, with the opening and the closing of the bedroom door. Then, to my surprise, there came a long silence, bro- ken only by the heavy breathings and gaspings of the sick man. I could imagine that our visitor was stand- ing by the bedside and looking down at the sufferer.
