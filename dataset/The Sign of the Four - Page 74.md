It is a point which is continually turning up in criminal trials, and which is sometimes of supreme importance as a clue. If you can say deﬁnitely, for example, that some murder has been done by a man who was smok- ing an Indian lunkah, it obviously narrows your ﬁeld of search. But I weary you with my hobby.” “Not at all,” I answered, earnestly. “It is of the greatest interest to me, especially since I have had the opportunity of observing your practical application of it.
